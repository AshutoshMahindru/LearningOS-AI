# Autonomy Controller

Control how much implementation AI may perform based on independent evidence, not calendar time.

Principle:
- early: AI explains/reviews; learner writes key implementation,
- middle: AI implements bounded components; learner specifies, tests, and explains,
- later: AI may implement subsystems; learner owns architecture, interfaces, evaluation, and review,
- advanced: multiple agents may build; learner owns system boundaries, trade-offs, critical reviews, and evidence.

If no-AI gates or transfer performance deteriorate, reduce autonomy.
