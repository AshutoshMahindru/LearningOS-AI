# M18 No-AI Transfer Gate

Complete this gate without AI-generated code or prose and without copying the
checkout notebook. A calculator and the Python standard library are allowed.

## Fresh scenario

A support team trials a new queue policy. The existing policy resolves 91 of 140
tickets within one day; the new policy resolves 101 of 140. The team also explored
response time, satisfaction, reopen rate, escalation rate, handle time, and nine
agent-level segments after seeing the primary result.

Produce a one-page decision note and a reproducible calculation that:

- names the population, sample, primary metric, estimand, and unit of analysis;
- reports each rate, the absolute and relative effect, standard error, and a 95%
  interval for the primary comparison;
- states a useful null hypothesis and interprets the test without saying that its
  p-value is the probability the null is true;
- distinguishes “evidence is inconclusive” from “the policies are equivalent”;
- identifies the post-hoc comparison family and proposes a defensible correction
  or a fresh confirmatory study;
- names at least two threats that the interval and test do not solve; and
- makes a decision that incorporates operational value and downside, not only a
  significance threshold.

Then explain the reasoning aloud from a blank page. Passing requires reproducible
numbers, calibrated language, and a defensible decision rule; a bare significant /
not-significant label does not pass.
