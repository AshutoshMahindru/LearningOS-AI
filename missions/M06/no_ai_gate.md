# M06 No-AI Gate — Fresh Question, Independent Chart Choice

Complete this gate without an AI assistant or AI-generated code. Use the fresh fixture at `datasets/M06/community_programs_fresh.csv`; do not reuse a chart from the guided support-ticket analysis by changing only column names.

## Fresh decision question

> Do weekend community programs appear to have a different no-show rate from weekday programs, and what should the library investigate before changing its schedule?

## Independent task

1. Define the row grain and calculate no-shows from `registered - attended`.
2. Write a prediction before calculating group results.
3. Choose a chart from the question and data types; explain why it answers the question better than at least one alternative.
4. Show both the no-show rate and the underlying registration count for weekday and weekend programs.
5. Check whether format, topic mix, or promotion channel could explain an apparent day-type difference.
6. Write three explicitly labelled statements:
   - **Visible observation**
   - **Inference**
   - **What cannot yet be concluded**
7. State at least three limitations, including one about sample size or representativeness and one about confounding.
8. Name the next data collection or comparison that would most reduce uncertainty.

## Rules

- Time box: 30–60 minutes.
- AI may not choose the chart, write the code, or draft the interpretation.
- Looking up basic library syntax is allowed only after the chart rationale is written.
- Do not fit a model.

## Pass standard

Pass requires a defensible chart choice, correct denominators, an interpretation grounded in visible evidence, and clear limits on what the fixture can establish. A polished chart without reasoning does not pass.
