# Mission M41: Design an Integrated AI System

## Overview
Mission M41 opens Phase P8 (Integration Capstone) and Flagship V11 (*Evaluated & Observable AI System*). Building upon M01–M40 components and the M40 evaluation suite (`m40.eval.v1`), M41 focuses on systems engineering and architectural design for integrated AI applications.

## Key Focus Areas
1. **System Boundaries**: System, Data, Control, and Trust boundaries.
2. **Decision Classifier**: Explicit separation between deterministic logic and non-deterministic model inference.
3. **Interface Contracts**: Strict contract schemas across Retrieval, Model, Tools, Memory, Workflow, and Evaluation.
4. **Degradation & Reliability**: Tiered fallback policies under system failure.
5. **Observability & Budgeting**: Token, latency, cost, and safety governance.
6. **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)**: Architectural blueprints for production deployment.
