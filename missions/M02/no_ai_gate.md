# M02 No-AI Gate — Fresh Supervised System

Complete this gate without AI-generated code, autocomplete, copied notebook cells, or an AI-authored explanation. Documentation and your own prior notes are allowed.

In a blank notebook or script, use `datasets/M02/wine.csv` to build a fresh supervised classifier. Choose and record a split seed, feature set, model, and at least one hyperparameter choice. The recipe must differ from the demonstrated baseline in at least two of those dimensions.

Submit:

1. a runnable raw-data → features/target → split → fit → predict → evaluate → interrogate system;
2. a prediction written before execution;
3. disjoint-row and prediction-count validation;
4. at least two metrics plus a confusion matrix or error table;
5. interrogation of at least three errors, or an explicit explanation if fewer exist;
6. identification of the fit, prediction, and evaluation boundaries;
7. one controlled label or evaluation failure, its diagnosis, repair, and verification;
8. a five-sentence explanation of what the model learned from, what it never saw, what the metric establishes, the system's limitations, and the next experiment.

The reviewer should be able to restart and run the submitted artifact without a secret or network connection. Passing requires fresh execution and explanation, not copying the baseline or reporting a score alone.
